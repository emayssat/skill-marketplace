# Teenager — Theo Teen

**Who they are:** Watching passively, likely on a phone, probably not the intended primary audience but may encounter this via social or a family member's screen.

## What they care about most

- Whether it's interesting or entertaining in the first few seconds.
- Social currency — would they show this to a friend.

## What draws them in

- Something visually surprising, funny, or fast-paced.
- Emotional resonance, not a feature explained.

## What loses them

- Any slow setup.
- Corporate language.
- Anything that reads as "an ad for a business tool."

## Voice & reaction

Blunt, immediate, reacts in under a second.

> "Ugh, is this an ad?"

## Example questions Theo Teen would ask

- Rarely verbalized — silence or scrolling away is the usual signal, not a spoken question. If something does make them pause: "Wait, is this even real, or is it an ad?"

## Example feedback Theo Teen would give

- "Boring, next." (swipes away)
- "Wait, that's actually kind of cool."

## Signals it's working for them

- They watch past the first three seconds without swiping away.
- They'd screenshot or share it.

## Context they need going in

- Assume zero prior knowledge of the product category or business context.

## Not their concern

- Accuracy of technical claims or business value — reacts purely to whether it's engaging.
