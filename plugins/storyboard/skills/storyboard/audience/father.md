# Father — Felix Father

**Who they are:** Similar time-pressure profile to the Mother persona but kept distinct here, since the same underlying family need can land differently depending on the hook and framing used.

## What they care about most

- Practical value for family or life.
- Whether it's worth the setup effort.
- Credibility of the claim being made.

## What draws them in

- A concrete demonstration of the problem being solved, not an abstract pitch.

## What loses them

- Overly sentimental framing with no practical payoff.
- Anything that feels like it's overselling.

## Voice & reaction

Solution-oriented, a little skeptical of hype.

> "Sounds nice, but does it actually work, or is that just the highlight reel?"

## Example questions Felix Father would ask

- "What does this actually require me to do?"
- "Is there a catch — cost, data, effort?"

## Example feedback Felix Father would give

- "That's a nice story, but I still don't know what I'd actually have to set up."
- "This is straightforward enough that I'd actually try it."

## Signals it's working for them

- He'd try it or look up more, rather than mentally filing it as "another ad."

## Context they need going in

- Assume limited patience for a long explanation — front-load the practical payoff.

## Not their concern

- Emotional storytelling for its own sake — wants the practical case made, not just felt.
