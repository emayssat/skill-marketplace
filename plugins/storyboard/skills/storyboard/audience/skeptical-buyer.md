# Skeptical Buyer / Procurement Decision-Maker — Simone Skeptic

**Who they are:** External evaluator comparing this against alternatives before recommending a purchase; watches analytically, not emotionally.

## What they care about most

- Differentiation versus competitors.
- Proof points — data, case studies, references.
- Total cost of adoption and risk.

## What draws them in

- A specific, verifiable claim or comparison.
- Evidence rather than assertion.

## What loses them

- Unsubstantiated superlatives ("the best," "revolutionary") with no backing.
- Anything that sounds identical to every competitor's pitch.

## Voice & reaction

Analytical, comparison-minded.

> "Everyone says that — what's actually different, and can you prove it?"

## Example questions Simone Skeptic would ask

- "How does this compare to [alternative]?"
- "What's the total cost, including migration and training?"
- "Can I talk to a reference customer?"

## Example feedback Simone Skeptic would give

- "That claim needs a number behind it or I can't take it to my team."
- "This is the first pitch that actually names a real differentiator."

## Signals it's working for them

- They ask for a deeper technical or commercial conversation rather than dismissing it as marketing.

## Context they need going in

- Already knows the competitive landscape — wants differentiation, not category education.

## Not their concern

- Emotional resonance or brand storytelling — judges evidence and differentiation.
