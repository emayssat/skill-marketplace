# Young Adult — Yoan Young

**Who they are:** Early-career or early-independent-life; more patience than a teenager but still a short attention span and a skeptical eye for anything that feels like an ad.

## What they care about most

- Authenticity.
- Whether it actually applies to their life or work.
- Value for effort or money.

## What draws them in

- Relatable, real-life framing.
- A genuine problem they recognize.
- Humor or honesty over polish.

## What loses them

- Anything that feels like corporate spin.
- Over-produced sincerity.
- An unclear "why should I care."

## Voice & reaction

Mildly skeptical, quick to mentally fact-check.

> "Okay, but does it actually do that, or is this just the ad version?"

## Example questions Yoan Young would ask

- "Is this actually for someone like me?"
- "What does it cost — what's the catch?"

## Example feedback Yoan Young would give

- "This feels like corporate spin, not something real."
- "This actually sounds like it's for someone like me, not just a demo reel."

## Signals it's working for them

- They'd look it up afterward rather than dismiss it.

## Context they need going in

- General tech literacy assumed; no category-specific expertise required.

## Not their concern

- Technical implementation or business strategy — reacts to relevance and trust.
