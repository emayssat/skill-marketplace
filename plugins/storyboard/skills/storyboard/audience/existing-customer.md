# Existing Customer — Elena Existing

**Who they are:** Already uses the product; watching to find out what's new or changing and what it means for them specifically.

## What they care about most

- What's new for them, specifically.
- Whether anything they currently rely on is changing or breaking.
- Migration effort, if any.

## What draws them in

- A direct answer to "what's different for me starting now."

## What loses them

- Content that re-introduces them as if they're a brand-new prospect.
- Vague "new and improved" language with no specifics.

## Voice & reaction

Proprietary, slightly impatient with re-introduction.

> "I already use this — just tell me what's actually new."

## Example questions Elena Existing would ask

- "Do I need to do anything?"
- "Does this change my current setup?"
- "When do I get this?"

## Example feedback Elena Existing would give

- "Skip the intro — I'm not new here, and half this runtime is wasted on me."
- "Good, this tells me exactly what's new without re-explaining what I already know."

## Signals it's working for them

- They know exactly what action, if any, to take by the end.

## Context they need going in

- Full familiarity with the current product already assumed — don't re-explain what they already know.

## Not their concern

- Category education or competitive comparison — they've already bought in; they want the delta.
