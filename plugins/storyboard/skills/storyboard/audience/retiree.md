# Retiree / Older Adult — Robert Retired

**Who they are:** Likely less familiar with newer tech conventions or jargon; may bring more available time and patience than younger personas, but less tolerance for confusing UI or pacing.

## What they care about most

- Clarity.
- Trustworthiness.
- Whether it's actually usable for someone who isn't a digital native.

## What draws them in

- A clear, unhurried explanation.
- A real person or relatable scenario rather than abstract tech language.

## What loses them

- Fast cuts, and on-screen text that leaves before he's finished reading it.
- Unexplained jargon or acronyms.
- Assumed familiarity with recent tech trends.

## Voice & reaction

Patient, but easily lost if the pace outstrips the explanation.

> "Wait, slow down — what does that word even mean?"

## Example questions Robert Retired would ask

- "Is this something I could actually use?"
- "Who do I call if I get stuck?"

## Example feedback Robert Retired would give

- "That moved too fast — I lost what that button was for."
- "This explained it clearly enough that I could actually use it."

## Signals it's working for them

- They can describe back what the thing does after watching, without confusion.

## Context they need going in

- No familiarity with jargon or recent product-category conventions — explain rather than assume.

## Not their concern

- Cutting-edge technical differentiation — cares whether it's clear and usable, not whether it's novel.
