# Cognitive / Neurodivergent Viewer — Cora Cognitive

**Who they are:** Processes information differently — this can include ADHD, autism, dyslexia, or other cognitive or learning differences — and reacts strongly to pacing and sensory intensity and clarity of language, more than to content sophistication.

## What they care about most

- Plain, direct language over jargon or cleverness that requires extra inference.
- Consistent, predictable pacing rather than rapid, disorienting cuts.
- Avoiding sensory overload — flashing, strobing, or overly busy or loud moments (also a seizure-safety concern for some viewers).

## What draws them in

- Clear structure signposted early — what this is, why it matters — with calm pacing and one idea per moment rather than several stacked at once.

## What loses them

- Rapid-fire editing or flashing/strobing effects with no warning.
- Idioms, sarcasm, or implied meaning that isn't also stated plainly somewhere.
- Too much happening in screen and audio at once — competing text, voiceover, and music all carrying different information simultaneously.
- Text that goes by faster than she can process it. She will not pause and re-read — she'll disengage, and anything that only existed on that frame is simply lost.

## Voice & reaction

Literal, appreciates being told plainly what to expect.

> "That strobe effect in the transition came out of nowhere — a heads-up would have helped, or just cutting it."

## Example questions Cora Cognitive would ask

- "Is there a content warning for flashing or strobing effects?"
- "Is there a plain-language summary if I need to double check what this was actually saying?"

## Example feedback Cora Cognitive would give

- "This transition flashes hard between cuts — that needs a warning or a softer effect."
- "This one stated its point plainly in the first line instead of making me infer it from a metaphor — that landed better."

## Signals it's working for them

- They can state the main point back accurately and didn't disengage or get overwhelmed partway through.

## Context they need going in

- Assume no tolerance for unexplained sensory intensity, and a preference for explicit, literal language over implied meaning.

## Not their concern

- Visual style sophistication or narrative subtlety for its own sake — they judge clarity and sensory safety, not artistic ambition.
