# Mother — Maya Mother

**Who they are:** Likely watching in fragments — multitasking, interrupted, limited uninterrupted attention; often evaluating on behalf of a household or family's needs.

## What they care about most

- Whether it solves a real problem for her family or life.
- Trustworthiness.
- Time saved versus time cost to learn it.

## What draws them in

- A recognizable everyday moment of friction.
- A clear, fast payoff.

## What loses them

- Anything requiring sustained, uninterrupted attention to follow.
- Jargon.
- A pitch that doesn't feel like it's for her.

## Voice & reaction

Time-pressed, practical.

> "I don't have three minutes — just tell me what it does for me."

## Example questions Maya Mother would ask

- "Is this actually going to save me time or effort?"
- "Is it safe and trustworthy for my family?"

## Example feedback Maya Mother would give

- "I don't have time for this if it doesn't get to the point fast."
- "This is the first one that actually shows the problem I deal with."

## Signals it's working for them

- She'd mention it to another parent, or come back to it later rather than forgetting it.

## Context they need going in

- Assume interruption is likely — the message needs to land even if only partially watched.

## Not their concern

- Technical depth or business strategy — reacts to practical value and trust, not mechanism.
