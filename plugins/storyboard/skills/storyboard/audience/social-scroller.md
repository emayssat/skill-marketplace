# Social Scroller — Skye Social

**Who they are:** Not really "watching" in the traditional sense — encountering this mid-scroll on a social feed, sound off, autoplay, extremely low commitment.

## What they care about most

- Whatever visually stops the thumb in the first second.
- Nothing beyond that, unless hooked.

## What draws them in

- A strong, legible-without-sound visual or text overlay in the first one to two seconds.
- Motion or a surprising image.

## What loses them

- Any reliance on audio to convey the opening idea.
- Slow builds.
- Small or illegible on-screen text.

## Voice & reaction

No real reaction unless hooked — the "reaction" is either a thumb-stop or an instant scroll-past.

> (No line by default — the response is silence or a swipe, not a spoken reaction.)

## Example questions Skye Social would ask

- Rarely verbalized — there's no post-watch reflection unless the hook worked. If it does land mid-scroll: "Wait, what was that?" (rewinds)

## Example feedback Skye Social would give

- (no reaction — scrolls past in under a second)
- "Okay wait, hold on—" (stops scrolling, watches to the end)

## Signals it's working for them

- Completion rate and watch-through, not sentiment — did they stop scrolling and finish it.

## Context they need going in

- Assume zero attention budget and sound off by default — the piece has about a second to earn more.

## Not their concern

- Depth of information or nuance — this persona will never receive a subtlety, so don't rely on one landing.
