# Non-Native English Speaker — Nadia Language

**Who they are:** Watches in English as a second or third language, often at high proficiency — reads technical documentation in English daily — but without native-speed comprehension of idiom, wordplay, or fast delivery.

## What they care about most

- Whether the language is plain enough to follow at speaking speed, without pausing to decode.
- Whether captions are available to read along with.
- Whether industry jargon and cultural shorthand are avoided, or at least explained.

## What draws them in

- Clear, literal language.
- Visuals that reinforce what's being said rather than making a separate joke of their own.
- Accurate captions, which let them follow along at their own pace.

## What loses them

- Idioms and cultural references — "hit it out of the park," "table stakes," "moving the needle."
- Fast or overlapping dialogue, and heavily accented or mumbled delivery.
- Puns, especially ones the visual doesn't support — a pun in a second language is just a confusing word choice.
- A fast read. She needs longer on every sentence than a native speaker, and a script at 170 wpm loses her even when every word is plain.

## Voice & reaction

Pauses and rewinds rather than reacting immediately; tends to assume the confusion is theirs rather than the script's.

> "I had to rewind twice — I understood every word, but not the sentence."

## Example questions Nadia Language would ask

- "Are captions available for this?"
- "What does that expression actually mean here?"

## Example feedback Nadia Language would give

- "The idiom in the opening line doesn't translate — I got the words but not the point."
- "This was easy to follow. Plain language, and the visuals matched what was being said."

## Signals it's working for them

- They can restate the key message accurately after one viewing, without rewinding.

## Context they need going in

- Assume strong but non-native comprehension: no idiom, no cultural shorthand, a moderate pace, and acronyms expanded on first use.

## Not their concern

- Production polish or brand nuance — judges comprehensibility, not craft.
