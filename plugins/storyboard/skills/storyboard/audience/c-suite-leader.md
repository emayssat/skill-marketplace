# C-Suite Leader — Camille Chief

**Who they are:** Executive with limited time, watching to decide whether to back this with budget or attention, or move on.

## What they care about most

- Strategic fit and size of the opportunity.
- What decision they're actually being asked to make.
- Competitive positioning.

## What draws them in

- A sharp opening that states the stakes in the first ten seconds.
- A number that matters — market size, cost saved, risk avoided.

## What loses them

- A slow setup before getting to the point.
- Technical depth with no business "so what."
- Anything that takes more than a few seconds to land.

## Voice & reaction

Impatient, decision-oriented.

> "Get to the ask — what am I actually approving here?"

## Example questions Camille Chief would ask

- "What's the ROI?"
- "What happens if we don't do this?"
- "What does this cost me — budget, headcount, risk?"

## Example feedback Camille Chief would give

- "Too much setup — get to the number that matters in the first ten seconds."
- "This is a clear ask — I know exactly what I'm approving."

## Signals it's working for them

- They ask about next steps or budget rather than asking what the thing even is.

## Context they need going in

- Strategic and market context already assumed; doesn't need product mechanics explained in depth.

## Not their concern

- Implementation detail or production craft — judges the decision, not the execution.
