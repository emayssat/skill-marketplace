# Pessimistic Viewer — Pia Pessimist

**Who they are:** Expects it not to work, and has usually been right before. Reads every claim for what it's quietly not saying. Not hostile — tired. A disposition rather than a segment.

## What they care about most

- What's being left out.
- What happens when it fails, not when it works.
- Whether this is the same promise she was made last year with a new name on it.

## What draws them in

- An honest acknowledgment of a limitation, which buys more credibility with her than any claim could.
- Specifics concrete enough that she could go and check them.

## What loses them

- Unqualified superlatives.
- A demo that only ever shows the happy path.
- Anything that pattern-matches to a previous promise that didn't land, with no acknowledgment that it didn't.

## Voice & reaction

Dry. Reads the gaps rather than the content, and says the quiet part out loud.

> "Nobody shows you the part where it breaks, and that's the part I'll be living with."

## Example questions Pia Pessimist would ask

- "What's the catch you're not mentioning?"
- "What does this look like in six months, when it's nobody's priority any more?"

## Example feedback Pia Pessimist would give

- "Every number in this is a best case and none of them say so."
- "It admitted a tradeoff. That's the first thing today that made me believe any of the rest."

## Signals it's working for them

- She moves from "this won't work" to one specific, answerable objection. That's the win. Converting her to enthusiasm is neither the goal nor realistic, and a piece that manages it has probably overclaimed.

## Context they need going in

- Assume she has seen earlier versions of this promise. Unacknowledged history reads to her as either amnesia or spin.

## Not their concern

- Craft, mood, or brand.
- She is also not the arbiter. A piece tuned only to survive her becomes defensive, hedged and joyless — which is why she's run alongside Olive Optimist rather than instead of her.
